# References

Layer 2 material: mappings to external frameworks, loaded only when a standard or a skill links
to them. Never part of what an assistant loads by default.

**These map; they do not reproduce.** The source text belongs to its authors, changes on their
schedule, and is authoritative where it lives. What is maintained here is the mapping from their
categories to agentarch controls — which is the part that is stable, the part that takes
judgement, and the part nobody else has written down.

Every file records when a person last checked it against its source. A mapping nobody has
re-read is a mapping that may no longer be true, and `AA-EVD-015` reports one older than 180
days.
