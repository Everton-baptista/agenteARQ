# ADR 0001 — Verification fixture

## Context
A fixture product exercised by the live verification tests; it has no runtime of its own.

## Options considered

### Isolated OCI verification
- Benefits: no network, read-only source, disposable workspace.
- Costs: needs Docker or Podman and a prepared image.

### Running checks on the host
- Benefits: nothing to install.
- Costs: checks see host credentials, sockets and the network.

## Decision
Isolated OCI verification, chosen by the maintainers for its isolation guarantees.

## Consequences
Verification requires a container runtime; that is the property under test.
