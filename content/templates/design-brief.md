# Design brief

## Subject

What the product is about, its domain, and the materials and vocabulary of that domain.

## Audience

Who uses it, how often, on which devices, and what they already trust.

## Main task

The one thing a user comes to do.

## References

Two or three products, places or objects the owner likes, and why.

## Bold element

The one thing this product will be remembered by.

## Vetoed looks

What the owner does not want to see.

## Direction

The direction chosen, who chose it and on which date, and the options it was compared with.
