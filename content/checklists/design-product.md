# Software design

- [ ] Inspect the existing architecture and the brief's constraints before naming components.
- [ ] Propose two to four technology options that fit the demand, each with benefits and costs.
- [ ] Label your recommendation as a recommendation; the owner chose among the compared options.
- [ ] Record context, options, the owner's decision and consequences in the ADR.
- [ ] Define interfaces, data ownership, authorization, and compatibility at actual boundaries.
- [ ] For UI, cover journeys, error states, keyboard access, labels, and responsiveness.
- [ ] Identify introduced risks, verification, and recovery without speculative layers.
