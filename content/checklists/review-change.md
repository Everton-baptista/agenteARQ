# Independent change review

- [ ] Reviewer is distinct from the author where independent review is required.
- [ ] Without a second person, review in a fresh-context read-only subagent recorded as `independent_agent`, stated as weaker than human review.
- [ ] Inspect requirements, actual diff, surrounding implementation, and test evidence.
- [ ] Prioritize reproducible correctness, security, compatibility, and coverage defects.
- [ ] Report each finding's trigger, consequence, location, severity, and uncertainty.
- [ ] Record the reviewed revision; recheck affected evidence after fixes.
- [ ] Give the owner five plain lines: changed, checked, found, remaining risk, decision needed.
