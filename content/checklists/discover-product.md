# Product discovery

- [ ] Inspect the existing brief, repository, and decisions; do not assume an AI product.
- [ ] Identify the user, problem, success measure, constraints, and exclusions.
- [ ] Record technology constraints and preferences (or "no preference") without choosing.
- [ ] Separate facts, cited research, and hypotheses.
- [ ] Select a useful vertical slice and record unresolved consequential decisions.
- [ ] Persist the accepted brief without implying external-action authorization.
