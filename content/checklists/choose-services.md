# Choosing services

- [ ] Start from the brief's constraints, and include "no new service" when it can work.
- [ ] Compare two to four options by cost model, lock-in, data residency, operating effort and free-tier traps.
- [ ] Read current pricing and terms now; date every fact and name its source.
- [ ] Label a recommendation; the owner chose.
- [ ] Record the ADR with sources, dates and the estimated monthly cost; secrets by name only.
- [ ] Never create accounts, accept terms or pay; those are the owner's actions.
